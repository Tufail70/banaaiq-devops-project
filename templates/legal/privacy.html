8. Your Rights

8.1 Under Indian DPDP Act 2023 (Data Principal Rights)

  • Right to Information — know what personal data is being processed and for what purpose.
  • Right to Correction & Erasure — correct inaccurate or incomplete data, or request erasure when no longer necessary.
  • Right to Grievance Redressal — lodge a complaint with BanaaIQ's Grievance Officer (see Section 8.3) within 30 days before escalating to the Data Protection Board of India.
  • Right to Nominate — nominate another individual to exercise rights on your behalf in case of death or incapacity.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent — withdraw consent at any time via account settings or email. Withdrawal does not affect lawfulness of processing prior to withdrawal.

8.2 Under Saudi Arabia PDPL 2021 (amended 2023)

  • Right to be informed about data collection
  • Right to access personal data held about you
  • Right to correct or update your data
  • Right to request destruction of your data
  • Right to object to data processing

12. India DPDP Act — Specific Compliance

Data Fiduciary Obligations Under the DPDP Act 2023, BanaaIQ acts as a Data Fiduciary. We meet these obligations:
  • Consent — Personal data is processed only on the basis of free, specific, informed, unconditional, and unambiguous consent. Consent is taken at registration and linked to each processing purpose.
  • Purpose Limitation — Data collected for account creation is not used for AI processing without separate disclosure (see Data & AI Disclosure page).
  • Data Minimisation — We collect only data necessary for the construction management features described in Section 3.
  • Storage Limitation — Retention periods are defined in Section 7. Deletion is automated.
  • Security Safeguards — TLS in transit, encrypted at rest on AWS Mumbai (ap-south-1). Breach notification within 72 hours.
  • Significant Data Fiduciary — BanaaIQ does not currently meet the thresholds for classification as a Significant Data Fiduciary. This section will be updated if we cross applicable thresholds.
