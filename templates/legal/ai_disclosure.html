Data & AI Disclosure

Last Updated: April 2025 Version v1.0 — Beta Release India & Saudi Arabia
Our Commitment We believe you have the right to know exactly how AI is used and what happens to your data. This document explains everything in plain language.

1. Purpose of This Disclosure

BanaaIQ is committed to full transparency about how Artificial Intelligence is used within our platform. This document explains what happens to your data when you use BanaaIQ's AI-powered features, which third-party AI providers are involved, and what risks you should be aware of.

2. AI Technology We Use

AI Provider Technology Purpose
OpenAI (USA) GPT-4o / GPT-4o mini Powers all AI features on BanaaIQ
Amazon Web Services Cloud Infrastructure (Mumbai) Hosts and stores all platform data

3. How AI Is Used in Each Feature

3.1 DPR Report Generation

  • You input: Site progress data, work completed, resources used, issues faced
  • AI processes: Structures data into a professional Daily Progress Report
  • Output: Formatted DPR report ready to share with project stakeholders
  • Data sent to OpenAI: The text you enter about your site's daily progress

3.2 BOQ Analysis

  • You input: Bill of Quantities data, material lists, quantities, cost information
  • AI processes: Analyzes quantities, identifies anomalies, provides cost insights
  • Output: AI-generated BOQ analysis report with recommendations
  • Data sent to OpenAI: BOQ text data and quantity details you enter

3.3 Inventory Management

  • You input: Material names, quantities, stock levels, supplier information
  • AI processes: Provides inventory insights, low-stock alerts, usage predictions
  • Output: Inventory recommendations and alerts
  • Data sent to OpenAI: Material names and quantity data entered in the system

3.4 Task Management

  • You input: Task descriptions, engineer assignments, deadlines, priorities
  • AI processes: Optimizes task assignments and provides progress insights
  • Output: Task recommendations and progress summaries
  • Data sent to OpenAI: Task descriptions and assignment details

4. What Data Goes to OpenAI

Data Type Sent to OpenAI? Notes
Text inputs for AI features YES Core AI processing requirement
Your name and email NO Stays within BanaaIQ only
Login credentials NO Encrypted, never shared
Payment information NO Not collected during beta
Device / IP information NO Stays within BanaaIQ only

5. OpenAI's Data Handling

  • OpenAI processes your text inputs and returns AI responses to BanaaIQ.
  • As of 2024, OpenAI does NOT use API data to train its models by default.
  • OpenAI may retain API inputs and outputs for up to 30 days for safety monitoring, then deletes them.
  • OpenAI's servers are located in the United States — data crosses international borders when you use AI features.
  • For more information, visit: openai.com/privacy
Cross-Border Notice By using BanaaIQ's AI features, you consent to your text inputs being processed by OpenAI's servers in the United States. This is a cross-border data transfer under Indian DPDP Act and Saudi PDPL.

6. AI Limitations & Risks

Risk What This Means for You
AI Hallucination AI may generate plausible-sounding but incorrect information. Always verify DPR and BOQ outputs with your engineering team before use.
Context Limitations AI does not fully understand your site's physical conditions, local regulations, or project-specific risks.
Not a Licensed Engineer AI output is NOT equivalent to advice from a licensed civil or structural engineer. It is a productivity tool only.
Data Quality AI output quality depends entirely on the data you input. Inaccurate inputs will produce inaccurate outputs.
Regulatory Compliance AI-generated BOQ and DPR must be reviewed for compliance with local construction regulations in India or Saudi Arabia before official use.
Critical Safety Notice All AI-generated content on BanaaIQ MUST be reviewed and approved by a qualified licensed engineer before being implemented on any construction site. BanaaIQ is a productivity and decision-support tool — NOT a replacement for professional engineering expertise.

7. Your Rights Regarding AI Processing

  • Right to know when AI is being used — BanaaIQ clearly labels all AI-generated content.
  • Right to opt out of AI features — manual data entry options are available.
  • Right to request deletion of your AI interaction data by emailing iqbaana@gmail.com .
  • Right to provide feedback on AI-generated content directly within the platform.
  • Right not to be subject to decisions made solely by automated AI without human review.

8. How We Minimize Data Shared with AI

  • We do not send your personal identity information (name, email, phone) to OpenAI.
  • We encourage users to avoid entering personally identifiable worker information in AI prompts.
  • We apply usage limits to prevent excessive data being sent to OpenAI.
  • We regularly review what data is sent to OpenAI to ensure minimum necessary sharing.

9. Future AI Features

BanaaIQ may introduce additional AI features in the future. Any new AI integrations will be disclosed to users before implementation, added to this Data & AI Disclosure document, and subject to user consent where required by law.
